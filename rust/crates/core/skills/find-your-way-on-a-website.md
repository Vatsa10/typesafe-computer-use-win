# Find your way on a website

Done means: you are on the page that holds what the user wants, confirmed by reading it, not by link text.

Order of attack. Move to the next only when the last failed, and never repeat a failed one:
1. Read the page you are on; the link is often there. locate for a hard-to-spot control.
2. Menu: open the main navigation and its submenus, observe.
3. Footer: scroll to the bottom. Policies, contact, careers, help, sitemap live there.
4. Site search: two or three plain keywords, then a synonym.
5. Sitemap: the footer link, or /sitemap.xml and /sitemap.
6. Web search: "site:domain terms" in a new tab; open the best result.
7. Back, then another route. Help center or FAQ often links the target.

After each move: observe, check the heading matches. Wrong page: go back once, take the next route. Do not go
more than three clicks from a known good page.

A cookie banner or overlay blocks the page: dismiss it with reject or close first. Login wall: stop and
tell the user. When every route failed, say which ones and give the closest page found.
